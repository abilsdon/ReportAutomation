Gender Swap

Swap gendered words in the open document.

Scope

Replacement rules

he → her · she → him · him → her · her → him
his → hers · hers → his · himself ↔ herself

Whole words only. Capitalisation and surrounding formatting are preserved.

Preview the document before applying changes.
Important

Save a copy first. “Her” and “his” can have more than one grammatical role, so review the result.
